## Twilight is a 2008 American romantic fantasy film directed by Catherine Hardwicke, based on the novel by Stephenie Meyer. The story follows teenager Bella Swan (Kristen Stewart) as she falls in love with the mysterious vampire Edward Cullen (Robert Pattinson) in Forks, Washington. The film, which is the first in The Twilight Saga series, focuses on their relationship and the danger posed by a rival coven of vampires
